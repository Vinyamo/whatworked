# Decision cards format

**When to suggest:** the audience is broad or under-specified, or readers fall into distinct
sub-populations ("which case am I?") — e.g. *just starting / first-line failed / wants drug-free /
severe*.

**Shape:**

1. A one-paragraph "find your card" intro.
2. 3–4 `## archetype cards`. Each card:
   - Who it's for (1–2 lines, concrete signals).
   - The **2–3 best moves for that situation**, each with its concrete `**How:**` line and statbar
     metrics line.
3. A shared `## What to stop doing` block at the end (applies across cards).

**Caution:** cards naturally de-emphasise the long tail — **still include the long-tail table at
the end** so alternatives aren't lost.

**Shared content rules** (all formats): every option uses the canonical statbar metrics line,
per-option `**How:**` and `**Science:**` lines, and 1–2 blockquoted quotes cited `— r/<sub>` —
see `STUDY_GUIDELINES.md` → "Breadth-mode report format" and "Per-option card & metrics".

**Always include:** `## What to stop doing` · long-tail table (real ratings, never a uniform "3") ·
`## Sources` appendix (one row per subreddit, posts and comments split) · demographics ·
missing-info questions · caveats (incl. the "Evidence ≠ proof" box) · key takeaways.
